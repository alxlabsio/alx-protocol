## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing and merging pull requests
- Managing releases and version numbering
- Responding to security disclosures (see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md))
- Maintaining the normative specification (`protocol/spec.md`)
- Ensuring conformance test vectors remain authoritative
- Moderating discussions and enforcing the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Becoming a Maintainer

The project is open to additional maintainers. To be considered:

1. Demonstrate sustained, high-quality contributions
2. Show understanding of the protocol's design principles
3. Be nominated by an existing maintainer

Maintainer decisions are made by consensus among existing maintainers.

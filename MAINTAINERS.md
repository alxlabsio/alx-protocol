Current maintainers of the ALX Protocol repository.

## Protocol Stewardship

| Name | Role | Affiliation | Contact |
|------|------|-------------|---------|
| ALX Foundation | Protocol steward and canonical maintainer | Independent nonprofit | [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/xandrlabs/alx-protocol/issues) |
| Xandr Labs | Founding contributor and implementation maintainer | [xandrlabs.ai](https://xandrlabs.ai) | team@xandrlabs.ai |

ALX Foundation maintains the open protocol and its canonical conformance materials independently of any commercial product, application, service, or economic mechanism. Xandr Labs develops implementations, tooling, applications, services, and supporting infrastructure for the ALX ecosystem. Xandr Labs commercial products and ecosystem services do not determine protocol conformance.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing and merging pull requests
- Managing releases and version numbering
- Responding to security disclosures (see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md))
- Maintaining the normative specification (`protocol/spec.md`)
- Ensuring conformance test vectors remain authoritative
- Moderating discussions and enforcing the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Becoming a Maintainer

The project is open to additional maintainers. To be considered:

1. Demonstrate sustained, high-quality contributions
2. Show understanding of the protocol's design principles
3. Be nominated by an existing maintainer

Maintainer nominations and decisions follow the stewardship and review process in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](./GOVERNANCE.md). While ALX Foundation is the single protocol steward, it retains final merge authority for canonical protocol materials.

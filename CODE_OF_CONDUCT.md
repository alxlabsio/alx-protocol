# Code of Conduct

This document defines conduct requirements for ALX Protocol project spaces. This document does not define protocol behavior.

---

## Purpose

This document defines:

* expected conduct;
* prohibited conduct;
* scope;
* reporting procedures;
* enforcement responsibilities;
* confidentiality requirements;
* conflict-of-interest requirements;
* enforcement records.

---

## Expected Conduct

Participants MUST:

* communicate professionally;
* provide technical feedback relevant to the work under review;
* address implementation, documentation, governance, or protocol issues without personal attacks;
* respect differing technical positions;
* identify assumptions and evidence when making technical claims;
* follow repository contribution and review processes;
* protect confidential security and personal information;
* comply with applicable repository policies.

Participants SHOULD:

* keep discussions within the scope of the issue, pull request, review, or governance process;
* distinguish factual claims from proposals or opinions;
* provide reproducible evidence for technical defects when available;
* request clarification when requirements or intent are unclear.

---

## Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is prohibited:

* harassment;
* threats;
* intimidation;
* discriminatory language or conduct;
* personal attacks;
* targeted insults;
* sustained disruption of project processes;
* deliberate misrepresentation of another participant's statements or work;
* publication of private information without authorization;
* publication of confidential security information before coordinated disclosure;
* use of project spaces for unrelated commercial solicitation, spam, or abuse;
* retaliation against a person for submitting a conduct or security report.

Technical disagreement, rejection of a proposal, review criticism, and enforcement of repository requirements do not constitute prohibited conduct when communicated professionally.

---

## Technical Review Conduct

Technical review MUST address the work rather than the person who submitted the work.

Review comments SHOULD identify applicable:

* requirement;
* affected file;
* protocol invariant;
* test result;
* conformance result;
* security concern;
* documentation issue;
* requested correction.

Reviewers MUST NOT use approval, merge, release, or repository permissions to punish technical disagreement.

Contributors MUST NOT treat required conformance, review, security, or governance checks as personal objections.

Protocol disputes MUST be handled through the applicable governance process.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to ALX Protocol project spaces, including:

* GitHub issues;
* pull requests;
* code reviews;
* GitHub Discussions;
* RFC discussions;
* repository-managed communication channels;
* project meetings;
* security-related communication;
* release coordination;
* maintainer communication conducted in an ALX Protocol capacity.

This policy also applies to conduct outside repository-managed spaces when a participant represents an official ALX Protocol role or when the conduct directly affects project participation or safety.

---

## Reporting

Conduct violations SHOULD be reported privately when the report contains personal, sensitive, or security-related information.

**Email:** `hello@alxlabs.io`

A report SHOULD include:

* description of the conduct;
* date or approximate date;
* affected project space;
* links or supporting records when available;
* names or account identifiers of involved participants when known;
* requested confidentiality constraints.

Reports SHOULD contain only information required for review.

Security vulnerabilities MUST be reported through [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md), not through the conduct-reporting process.

---

## Confidentiality

Maintainers handling conduct reports MUST protect:

* reporter identity when confidentiality is requested;
* private contact information;
* sensitive personal information;
* confidential security information;
* internal enforcement discussion when disclosure would create additional harm.

Information MAY be disclosed when required to investigate the report, enforce repository policy, comply with law, or protect participants.

The reporter SHOULD be informed before identifiable information is shared when operationally possible.

Public enforcement records MUST NOT include unnecessary private information.

---

## Enforcement Responsibility

ALX Foundation holds protocol stewardship responsibility.

ALX Labs MAY administer operational conduct intake and repository moderation under authority assigned by ALX Foundation.

Maintainers responsible for conduct enforcement MAY:

* request corrective action;
* remove or edit prohibited content where repository permissions allow;
* restrict participation;
* temporarily suspend repository access;
* revoke maintainer or contributor privileges;
* refer a matter to the protocol steward;
* close or lock discussions;
* document an enforcement decision.

Repository permissions do not grant authority to alter protocol behavior or conformance requirements.

Conduct enforcement MUST NOT be used to bypass repository governance, security, or conformance processes.

---

## Enforcement Process

A conduct report SHOULD be handled through the following process:

1. acknowledge receipt;
2. identify the applicable project space and participants;
3. preserve relevant records;
4. determine whether recusal is required;
5. review the reported conduct;
6. request additional information when required;
7. determine whether this policy was violated;
8. determine the applicable response;
9. notify affected participants as appropriate;
10. record the enforcement outcome.

Enforcement decisions SHOULD identify the policy provision applied and the action taken.

Enforcement records MUST exclude unnecessary private information.

---

## Enforcement Actions

Enforcement actions MAY include:

### Correction

A participant is required to correct conduct or content that violates this policy.

Examples include:

* editing an inappropriate comment;
* removing private information;
* stopping disruptive behavior.

### Warning

A participant receives a documented warning identifying the prohibited conduct and required correction.

### Participation Restriction

A participant's ability to participate in specified project spaces is restricted for a defined period or scope.

### Access Removal

Repository, discussion, review, or maintainer access is revoked.

### Permanent Exclusion

A participant is prohibited from participating in ALX Protocol project spaces.

The enforcement action MUST address the documented conduct violation.

Enforcement severity SHOULD consider:

* impact;
* recurrence;
* intent when relevant;
* prior enforcement history;
* risk to participants;
* risk to project operations;
* cooperation with corrective action.

---

## Maintainer Conduct

Maintainers are subject to the same conduct requirements as other participants.

Maintainers MUST:

* use repository authority within assigned responsibilities;
* communicate enforcement decisions professionally;
* protect confidential reports;
* document material enforcement actions;
* disclose applicable conflicts of interest;
* preserve required governance and review processes.

Maintainers MUST NOT:

* retaliate against reporters;
* use repository authority to suppress legitimate technical disagreement;
* disclose confidential conduct reports without operational need;
* use conduct enforcement to alter protocol requirements;
* use maintainer status to exempt themselves from this policy.

Maintainer responsibilities are also defined in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](./MAINTAINERS.md).

---

## Conflict of Interest

A person handling a conduct report MUST disclose a material conflict of interest related to the report.

A material conflict includes:

* direct involvement in the reported conduct;
* a direct reporting relationship with an involved participant;
* a financial or contractual interest in the outcome;
* another relationship that prevents impartial review.

A person with a material conflict MUST recuse from the enforcement decision.

Another authorized maintainer or the protocol steward MUST perform the review.

Affiliation with ALX Foundation or ALX Labs does not independently require recusal.

---

## False or Abusive Reports

A report is not a policy violation because the reported conduct is not substantiated.

Good-faith reports MUST NOT result in retaliation.

Knowingly false reports, fabricated evidence, or repeated use of the reporting process to harass another participant MAY constitute a violation of this policy.

A finding that evidence is insufficient does not establish that a report was knowingly false.

---

## Appeals

A participant subject to a material enforcement action MAY request review by the protocol steward.

An appeal SHOULD identify:

* the enforcement decision;
* the reason for requesting review;
* relevant facts or evidence not addressed in the original decision.

The appeal reviewer MUST NOT be the sole person responsible for the original decision when another authorized reviewer is available.

An appeal does not automatically suspend an active safety or access restriction.

The appeal outcome SHOULD be documented.

---

## Enforcement Records

Material enforcement actions SHOULD have an internal record.

The record SHOULD identify:

* date;
* applicable policy provision;
* involved project space;
* enforcement action;
* reviewer;
* appeal status when applicable.

Public disclosure of an enforcement action is not required.

Public disclosure MAY occur when required to explain repository access changes, protect project participants, or maintain project records.

Public records MUST exclude unnecessary personal or confidential information.
